Build settings

Intensity controls how easily rising tension enters BUILDUP; decay sets how long BUILDUP holds when tension dips.

Drop settings

A drop fires on a sudden loudness/bass slam, scaled automatically by the Genre Agent.

Drop hit settings (Ch 7)

Channel 7 goes to 255 at the instant DROP starts, holds, then cuts or fades to 0.

Breakdown settings

Controls how quickly missing kick/bass enters BREAKDOWN (automatically widened for syncopated genres like Hip-Hop & DnB).

4/4 lock settings (Ch 8)

Channel 8 stays at 255 while a steady 4/4 kick grid is locked in the music.

Unattended / Server settings

Silence blackout, startup output state, and optional scheduled host reboots for unattended venue operation.
