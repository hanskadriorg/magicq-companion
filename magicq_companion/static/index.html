Build settings

Intensity is how easily a rising mix becomes BUILDUP. Decay is how long BUILDUP hangs on after that tension dips — raise it if builds cut out too early.

Drop settings

A drop is a sudden jump in loudness with kick/bass present. Intensity makes hits easier or harder; the rest is how the hit is measured.

Drop hit settings

Channel 9 goes to 255 at the instant DROP starts. Hold is how long it stays full; fade is how long it then ramps to 0.

Breakdown settings

A breakdown is the kick dropping out of the mix. Intensity is how easily that happens; beats is how long the kick must stay gone.

4/4 settings

Channel 11 stays at 255 while a 4/4 kick grid is locked in the music, and 0 when it isn’t. Sensitivity is how easily that lock happens; smoothing delays going on and coming off.

Server settings

These apply on this machine: silence blackout, whether output starts on, and an optional weekly reboot. Saving a reboot schedule writes crontab; it does not reboot now.
